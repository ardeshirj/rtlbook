# RTLBook's name and logo

The code is under the Apache License 2.0 (`LICENSE`), which gives no rights to the project's name or logo (its
section 6). This file says what those are and how they may be used.

## What the brand is
- **The names** RTLBook, written as one word with that capitalisation ("RTL" then "Book"), and FarsiEPUB, whose product RTLBook is ("RTLBook, by FarsiEPUB").
- **The logo:** an eight-pointed star of two squares, one turned 45°, with a smaller star inside and one saffron dot at its
  centre, in oxblood, and the name beside it ("RTL" in regular, "Book" in bold, in ink).

- **FarsiEPUB's logo:** its star, with one saffron dot, and the wordmark "FarsiEPUB".

## Using them
- **Builds of this project, unmodified,** show them as they are.
- **Anyone may name the project** when writing about it, linking to it or saying their work is built on it or works with it.
- **A version with changes, shipped to others,** uses its own name and logo, and does not present itself as RTLBook or as made by FarsiEPUB.
- **Not without asking:** the name or logo on another product, or in a way that suggests endorsement.

Questions: the maintainer, through the repository.
